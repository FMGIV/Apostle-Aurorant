Description:
"I was once the center of worlds, and now I've hit rock bottom. But then again, the good thing about the bottom... is that you could only rise."
What if that war had been different? How would history have turned out? Play as the New King who survived the battle in the 1940s. Traveling across borders, serving the candle-wielding people, bringing about the second dawn, or exploring more possibilities you never imagined - take action, all histories focuses on you.
